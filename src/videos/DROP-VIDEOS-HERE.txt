  03.mp4  (or 03-anything.mp4)  -> Profile 03 background video (muted)

Notes:
- All background videos automatically play MUTED in a smooth loop at subtle low opacity
  behind the Hero profile & 3D cards (your profile .mp3 music stays crystal clear!).

- "04-sample-theme.mp4" is the sample video for Profile 04. Dropping a new "04.mp4"
  automatically overrides it!
